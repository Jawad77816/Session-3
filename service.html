Local Expertise You Can Count On

Why a professional valuation makes the difference

Online home value estimates can provide a quick snapshot, but they often miss the important details that influence your property's true market value. Unlike automated tools such as Zillow's Zestimate, my valuations are based on local market knowledge, current trends, comparable properties, and the unique features of your home.

With a professional comparative market analysis (CMA), you receive a more accurate and personalized understanding of what your home could be worth — helping you make confident decisions about your next move.

Automated estimate vs. a professional CMA

  • Real local market knowledgeInsight an algorithm simply can't replicate.
  • Recent comparable salesPriced against homes that actually sold near you.
  • Your home's unique featuresUpgrades and character that estimates overlook.
