The story

Built on care,
not on volume.

Giana Lewis-Fairley spent more than a decade in medicine before turning her focus to aesthetics—and that clinical foundation is exactly what sets her work apart. She understands facial anatomy, she respects safety, and she knows that the best cosmetic result is one nobody can quite pinpoint.

She founded Aesthetics Med & Wellness by G to practice the way she always believed care should feel: unhurried consultations, honest recommendations (including "let's wait" when that's the right answer), and a plan built entirely around your face and goals—never a menu of upsells.

Today she treats clients across Rhode Island from Johnston and East Providence, and the response has been the same from the start: people leave looking like themselves, only more rested and more confident.
