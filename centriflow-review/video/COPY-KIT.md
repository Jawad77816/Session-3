### Posting tips
- **Upload the MP4 natively** (don't post a YouTube/Drive link — native video gets far more reach).
- LinkedIn feed video specs: MP4, 16:9, ≤5 min, ≤5GB — this file (13MB) is well within limits.
- Put the CTA link (`gocentriflow.ai`) in the **first comment** too; some feeds suppress reach on posts with outbound links in the body.
- Best-performing length on LinkedIn is <60s — this is 56s. ✅
